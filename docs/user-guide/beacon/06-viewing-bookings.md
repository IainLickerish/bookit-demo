# The Beacon Dental bookings list

Use the Beacon admin account to sign in, then open **Clinic bookings** at `/admin`.
Choose a **clinician**, date or status and apply the filters. The date follows
Europe/London time. If no bookings appear, clear a filter or select another day.

The table shows patient names and booking status. Keep access to authorised
Beacon staff. Patient accounts use **My bookings** to view only their own dental
appointments and cannot open the clinic admin list.

## Blocking time away

From **Clinic bookings**, open **Blocked time**. Choose a clinician, a
future start and end in the clinic's time zone, and an optional reason of up to
300 characters. Keep the reason brief, without personal or medical details.
Times repeated or skipped when the clocks change cannot be used; choose another
time. The end must be after the start.
If the details are invalid, the page explains what to correct and keeps your
clinician, start, end and reason. Nothing is saved or cancelled.

Choose **Review blocked time**. Check the clinician, the start and end,
and every affected appointment, including its client, time and duration. Any
confirmed appointment overlapping the block, even partly, will be cancelled.
An appointment ending exactly at the block's start or starting exactly at its end
is unaffected. **Change details** returns to your entries without saving.

Confirm once to save the blocked time and all affected cancellations together.
If the affected bookings change before you confirm, nothing is saved. Review
again to see the current impact. Clients cannot book or move into blocked time,
and cancelled appointments appear in their **My bookings** list. These staff
cancellations are not marked as late client cancellations.

No cancellation emails are sent at this stage. Arrange contact with affected
clients using your clinic's existing process.

Open a block under **Saved blocks** to revisit its time, reason and cancelled
bookings. Saved blocks appear 20 at a time, newest first. Choose **View older
blocks** to reach earlier entries, or **View newest blocks** to return to the start.
An older block can also be reopened from its saved link. A block with no affected
bookings says so. Cancelled bookings appear 20 at a time, earliest first. Choose
**View more cancellations** to see the next page, or **View first cancellations**
to return to the start. Every cancellation remains available when you reopen the
block. Only your clinic's staff can
create or view these blocks. This page does not offer editing or removal of saved
blocks.

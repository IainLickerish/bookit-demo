# The Beacon Dental bookings list

Use the Beacon admin account to sign in, then open **Clinic bookings** at `/admin`.
Choose a **clinician**, date or status and apply the filters. The date follows
Europe/London time. If no bookings appear, clear a filter or select another day.

The table shows patient names and booking status. Keep access to authorised
Beacon staff. Patient accounts use **My bookings** to view only their own dental
appointments and cannot open the clinic admin list.

## Blocking out time

In **Clinic bookings**, choose **Block out time**. Choose the clinician,
then enter **Start** and **End** in the clinic's displayed time zone. The end must
be after the start. Add a **Reason (optional)** if useful, then choose
**Save blocked time**. Check the saved name, period and reason below the form.
Times skipped or repeated during a clock change cannot be used; choose an
unambiguous time before or after the change.

Clients cannot book or move into any appointment that overlaps the blocked period.
An appointment ending exactly at its start, or starting exactly at its end, remains
available. Existing bookings stay confirmed. A warning under the saved period
lists any overlapping confirmed bookings with the client's name, time and booking
reference. Contact those clients to arrange another time. Only this clinic's staff
can see blocked periods, reasons and affected bookings.

# Welcome to Beacon Dental

BookIt helps you arrange appointments with your dentist or hygienist at Beacon
Dental. Sign in at `/auth/sign-in` with the account your clinic gave you. Use
**Book** to find a time and **My bookings** to see your appointments.

Times are shown in Europe/London and adjust for the seasonal clock change.
Need a hand? Contact Bella Byrne, Reception Lead, at `bella@beacondental.test`.
We can help Monday to Saturday, 08:00 to 18:00, Europe/London. Please sign out
when you have finished on a shared device.

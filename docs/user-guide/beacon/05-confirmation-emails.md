# Your Beacon Dental confirmation

Look for booking and rescheduling confirmations from `noreply@beacondental.test`.
They include your clinician and appointment date and time. Beacon does not include
patient contact details in confirmation messages.

This is a fictional demonstration: messages go to the local Mailpit inbox rather
than a real mail service. If a message is missing, check **My bookings** at
`/bookings` for the current appointment. For help, ask Bella Byrne at
`bella@beacondental.test`.

Booking and move emails include a `booking.ics` calendar file with the clinic,
practitioner, date, time and appointment length. Open it in Google Calendar, Outlook
or Apple Calendar. The calendar shows the correct UK time when set to Europe/London;
if you travel, your calendar may show the equivalent local time.

If email delivery fails, your appointment remains saved. You can still choose
**Add to calendar** beside it in **My bookings**. Calendar entries are copies, so
replace the old entry after a move and remove it yourself after cancelling.

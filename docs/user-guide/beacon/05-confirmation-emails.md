# Your Beacon Dental confirmation

Look for booking and rescheduling confirmations from `noreply@beacondental.test`.
They include your clinician and appointment date and time. Beacon does not include
patient contact details in confirmation messages.

This is a fictional demonstration: messages go to the local Mailpit inbox rather
than a real mail service. If a message is missing, check **My bookings** at
`/bookings` for the current appointment. For help, ask Bella Byrne at
`bella@beacondental.test`.

## Reminder emails

When the clinic has reminders switched on, an unchanged confirmed appointment booked
at least 24 hours ahead receives one reminder about 24 hours before its start.
The reminder shows the clinic, day, date, 24-hour local time and clinician.
Its reschedule and cancel links take you to **My bookings**, where you must sign in
to your own account before making a change. Opening a link does not change a booking.

Cancelled bookings and bookings made less than 24 hours ahead receive no reminder.
If a reminder is missing, check **My bookings** for your current appointment details.

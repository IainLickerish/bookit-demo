# Your Beacon Dental confirmation

Look for booking and rescheduling confirmations from `noreply@beacondental.test`.
They include your clinician and appointment date and time. Beacon does not include
patient contact details in confirmation messages.

This is a fictional demonstration: messages go to the local Mailpit inbox rather
than a real mail service. If a message is missing, check **My bookings** at
`/bookings` for the current appointment. For help, ask Bella Byrne at
`bella@beacondental.test`.

## Add to your calendar

After booking or moving, choose **Add to calendar** beside the confirmed appointment
in **My bookings**. Choose **Copy calendar details**. If copying is unavailable,
select and copy the visible details yourself, or type them into your calendar.
The booking and move emails include these same details as text, without an attachment.
The calendar summary contains the clinic, clinician, start, end, time zone and
duration. It leaves out your contact details and private booking links.

- In Google Calendar, choose **Create**, then **Event**.
- In Outlook, open Calendar and choose **New event**.
- In Apple Calendar, create a new event and open its details.

In each calendar, enter the title, start and end shown. Use the stated time zone
(`Europe/London` for UK bookings), so winter and summer times stay correct. Save
the event. Copying details does not create an event automatically.

If you move an appointment, update your existing calendar event to the new time yourself.
If you cancel, remove the event yourself. BookIt does not update your calendar.

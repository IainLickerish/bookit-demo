# Choosing a different time

Open **My bookings** at `/bookings` and choose **Reschedule** next to your upcoming
appointment. Pick a free date and time, then confirm. Check that the new time
appears in your bookings list.

Beacon Dental allows up to **2 reschedules per booking**. The new appointment must
be in the future and within the booking horizon. If someone else takes your new
slot first, your existing appointment stays booked. Please contact reception
when you need help after reaching the limit.

After moving, choose **Add to calendar** in **My bookings** for the new details.
Your move confirmation email also includes the updated details as text. Update
the existing event in your calendar yourself. BookIt does not update it for you.

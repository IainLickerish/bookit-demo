# Booking at Beacon Dental

Our appointments last **30 minutes**. Sign in and open **Book** at `/slots`.
Pick your clinician and date, then choose a free time. Check the details and
confirm your appointment. You can leave the phone number blank.

You can choose slots up to 220 days ahead, shown in Europe/London time. If another
patient takes your selected slot first, pick a different one. Once confirmed,
your dental appointment will appear in **My bookings** at `/bookings`.

# Cancelling your dental appointment

Open **My bookings** at `/bookings`, find your appointment and select **Cancel**.
You can also follow **Cancel** in a reminder email. Sign in if asked; you return
to the cancellation review. Only the booked client can open it.

Check the clinician, day, date, time and duration. The review explains
whether this counts as a late cancellation and that the time will become free.
Opening this page leaves your booking in place. Choose **Keep my booking** to
leave it unchanged, or **Confirm cancellation** to cancel it.

After confirmation, check the cancellation notice and the **Cancelled** status
in the past and cancelled list. Late cancellations are saved and marked as late.
The time becomes available to another client. If the booking or its late
cancellation status changed while you were reviewing it, review it again before
confirming.

At Beacon Dental, cancelling less than **48 hours** before the appointment counts
as a late cancellation. Exactly 48 hours is not late. Once the appointment has
started, contact reception instead of trying to cancel it in BookIt.

# Cancelling your dental appointment

Go to **My bookings** at `/bookings`, find your appointment and choose **Cancel**.
Look for it in the past and cancelled list afterwards. Cancelling releases the
slot so another patient can use it.

At Beacon Dental, cancelling less than **48 hours** before the appointment counts
as a late cancellation. Exactly 48 hours is not late. Once the appointment has
started, contact reception instead of trying to cancel it in BookIt.

If you cancel late, look for **Cancelled (late)** in your past and cancelled list.
Reception sees the same status, and another patient can book the time. If the
clinic changes its late-cancellation window, the current setting applies.

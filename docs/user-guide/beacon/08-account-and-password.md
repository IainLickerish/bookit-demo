# Your Beacon Dental account

Sign in at `/auth/sign-in` with your registered Beacon Dental email address.
Keep your password to yourself and sign out when using a shared computer.

Forgotten your password? Go to `/account/reset-password` and enter your registered
email address. Follow the email instructions to choose a new password. Bella
Byrne can help with account questions at `bella@beacondental.test`, Monday to
Saturday, 08:00 to 18:00, Europe/London.

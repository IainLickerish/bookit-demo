# Getting started with Acme Physio

BookIt helps you arrange sessions with your physiotherapist at Acme Physio.
Sign in at `/auth/sign-in` using the account supplied by the clinic. Choose **Book**
to find a session or **My bookings** to review your appointments. Times are shown
in Europe/London, including changes between summer and winter time.

For help, contact Alice Archer, Practice Manager, at `alice@acmephysio.test`.
Our support hours are Monday to Friday, 09:00 to 17:30, Europe/London.
Sign out when you finish, particularly on a shared device.

## Reminder email setting for staff

Sign in with your clinic staff account, open **Clinic bookings**, then **Clinic
settings**. Choose **On** or **Off** under **Reminder emails** and select **Save
settings**. Reopen settings to check the saved choice. Reminders start off and
only your clinic’s staff can change this choice.

When reminders are on, each upcoming confirmed booking is checked every 10 seconds
and can receive one reminder within 24 hours of its start. The reminder uses the
latest saved time and practitioner and shows UK time. A booking made inside that
window receives its reminder at the next check. Cancelled and started bookings
receive no reminder. Switching reminders off stops later checks from sending.

A reminder whose delivery cannot be confirmed is not sent again, even after a
move. Check **My bookings** for the latest booking details. Reminder emails include
separate **Reschedule** and **Cancel** links. Sign in with the booked client’s
account to review the current booking. Opening a link changes nothing; a change
requires your confirmation.
Booking confirmation emails continue as before.

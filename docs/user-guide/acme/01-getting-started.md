# Getting started with Acme Physio

BookIt helps you arrange sessions with your physiotherapist at Acme Physio.
Sign in at `/auth/sign-in` using the account supplied by the clinic. Choose **Book**
to find a session or **My bookings** to review your appointments. Times are shown
in Europe/London, including changes between summer and winter time.

For help, contact Alice Archer, Practice Manager, at `alice@acmephysio.test`.
Our support hours are Monday to Friday, 09:00 to 17:30, Europe/London.
Sign out when you finish, particularly on a shared device.

# Your Acme Physio confirmation

Booking and rescheduling confirmations are sent from `noreply@acmephysio.test`.
They describe the physiotherapist, session date and local time. Acme includes
contact details when available; providing a phone number is optional.

Use the attached `physio-session.ics` file to add your session to your calendar.
The entry shows Acme Physio, your physiotherapist, the UK date and time and
how long your session lasts. You can use the file you download from **My bookings**
in the same way.

- In Google Calendar on a computer, open **Settings**, then **Import & export**, and import the file. [Google Calendar help](https://support.google.com/calendar/answer/37118)
- In Outlook on the web, open **Add calendar**, then **Upload from file**. [Outlook help](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/outlook/import-or-subscribe-to-a-calendar-in-outlook-com-or-outlook-on-the-web)
- In Apple Calendar on a Mac, choose **File**, then **Import**. [Apple Calendar help](https://support.apple.com/guide/calendar/icl1023/mac)

If you move or cancel your session, check the old entry in your calendar and
remove it if it is still there.

In this fictional demonstration, email is delivered to the local Mailpit inbox,
not to a real email service. **My bookings** at `/bookings` is the place to check
your current appointment status if an email is missing. Ask Alice Archer at
`alice@acmephysio.test` for clinic assistance.

# Your Acme Physio confirmation

Booking and rescheduling confirmations are sent from `noreply@acmephysio.test`.
They describe the physiotherapist, appointment date and local time. Acme includes
contact details when available; providing a phone number is optional.

In this fictional demonstration, email is delivered to the local Mailpit inbox,
not to a real email service. **My bookings** at `/bookings` is the place to check
your current appointment status if an email is missing. Ask Alice Archer at
`alice@acmephysio.test` for clinic assistance.

## Add to your calendar

After booking or moving, choose **Add to calendar** beside the confirmed session
in **My bookings**. Choose **Copy calendar details**. If copying is unavailable,
select and copy the visible details yourself, or type them into your calendar.
The booking and move emails include these same details as text, without an attachment.
The calendar summary contains the clinic, physiotherapist, start, end, time zone and
duration. It leaves out your contact details and private booking links.

- In Google Calendar, choose **Create**, then **Event**.
- In Outlook, open Calendar and choose **New event**.
- In Apple Calendar, create a new event and open its details.

In each calendar, enter the title, start and end shown. Use the stated time zone
(`Europe/London` for UK bookings), so winter and summer times stay correct. Save
the event after checking that its length matches the duration shown. Enter each
detail in the matching calendar field. Copying details does not create an event
automatically.

If you move a session, update your existing calendar event to the new time yourself.
If you cancel, remove the event yourself. BookIt does not update your calendar.

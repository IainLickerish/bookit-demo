# Your Acme Physio confirmation

Booking and rescheduling confirmations are sent from `noreply@acmephysio.test`.
They describe the physiotherapist, appointment date and local time. Acme includes
contact details when available; providing a phone number is optional.

In this fictional demonstration, email is delivered to the local Mailpit inbox,
not to a real email service. **My bookings** at `/bookings` is the place to check
your current appointment status if an email is missing. Ask Alice Archer at
`alice@acmephysio.test` for clinic assistance.

Each booking and move confirmation includes an `appointment.ics` calendar file.
Open it in Google, Outlook or Apple Calendar to add your session. It includes
the clinic, physiotherapist, UK time and full session length. You can also choose
**Add to calendar** after booking or moving, or beside a confirmed booking in
**My bookings**. Only you can download your own calendar file while signed in.

The file is a copy of the saved session, not a live calendar subscription.
If you move or cancel, update your calendar too. Download the latest file from
**My bookings** after a move. If a download or email fails, your booking stays saved.

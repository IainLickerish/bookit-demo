# Your Acme Physio account

Use the email address registered with Acme Physio to sign in at `/auth/sign-in`.
Keep your password private and sign out of shared devices after each session.

If you forget your password, visit `/account/reset-password` and enter your
registered email address. Follow the instructions in the reset email to choose
a new password. If you need help with your account, contact Alice Archer at
`alice@acmephysio.test` during Monday to Friday, 09:00 to 17:30, Europe/London.

# Booking a physiotherapy session

1. Sign in and choose **Book**. Select your physiotherapist and date, then select **Show times**.
2. Use **Previous day** or **Next day** to look at another date. Check the date above the available times and select a time.
3. Check the physiotherapist, date, time and duration under **Confirm your booking**. To go back to **Book**, select
   **Choose a different time**.
4. You can leave the phone number blank. Select **Confirm booking** to book your session.

Acme Physio sessions are **45 minutes**. You can book up to 220 days ahead, and all times are UK local times.
If somebody books your chosen time first, choose another time. Your confirmed session appears under **My bookings**.

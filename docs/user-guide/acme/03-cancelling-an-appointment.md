# Cancelling an Acme Physio session

Open **My bookings** at `/bookings`, find the session and select **Cancel**.
Check that it moves to **Past and cancelled**. A cancelled time becomes
available for another client, including after a late cancellation.

Acme Physio treats cancellations made less than **24 hours** before the start as
late cancellations and shows them as **Cancelled (late)**. At exactly 24 hours
the cancellation is not late. If your session has already started, contact the
clinic for help.

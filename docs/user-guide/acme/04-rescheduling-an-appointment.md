# Moving your physiotherapy session

In **My bookings** at `/bookings`, select **Reschedule** beside an upcoming session.
Choose an available date and time, then confirm the move. Check the new time in
your bookings list. Your old slot is released only when the move succeeds.

Acme Physio allows up to **3 reschedules per booking**. The new time must be in
the future and within the booking horizon. If a time is taken while you are
choosing, your existing booking stays in place. Contact the clinic for help once
you reach the reschedule limit.

After a move, choose **Add to calendar** on the confirmation or in **My bookings**
to save the new time. A new confirmation email also includes a calendar file.
Check that your calendar shows the new time and remove the old event if it remains.
Calendar entries do not stay in sync with BookIt. Remove cancelled bookings from your calendar.

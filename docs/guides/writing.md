# Writing for BookIt

Each rule has a short ID (WR-1 and so on) so a review can name it. The rules apply to screens, emails and the clinics'
user guides alike.

## Voice

Sound like the receptionist at a good private clinic: warm, calm, assured and brief. Speak to the reader as "you" and
about the clinic by its name. Acme Physio is formal and concise; Beacon Dental is warmer and plain. Premium means
confident and specific, never flowery or full of hype.

## Rules

- **WR-1 UK English**: "cancelled", "organise", "colour".
- **WR-2 Short and plain**: everyday words and short sentences. No jargon, jokes, exclamation marks or marketing
  language. "Slot" is fine in the app; "database", "server" and error codes are not.
- **WR-3 Sentence case** for headings and buttons: "Confirm your booking", not "Confirm Your Booking". The short
  eyebrow labels above page titles are the only capitals (see the look-and-feel guide).
- **WR-4 Buttons say what they do**, starting with a verb: "Confirm booking", "Cancel booking", "Save settings". Never
  "OK" or "Submit".
- **WR-5 Messages after an action** are one short sentence ending in a full stop: "Booking cancelled."
- **WR-6 Problems** say what happened, then what to do. Say "Sorry" when it is not the reader's fault: "Sorry, that
  slot has just been booked. Please choose another."
- **WR-7 Dates and times** look like "Tue, 20 Oct 2026, 11:15", with a 24-hour clock, in the clinic's time zone. Day
  headings may spell out the day's name. Numbers with units are digits: "45 minutes".
- **WR-8 No em dashes.** Use a full stop, a comma or a colon instead.

## Each clinic's words

Use the words each clinic already uses with its clients:

|                 | Acme Physio     | Beacon Dental |
| --------------- | --------------- | ------------- |
| An appointment  | session         | appointment   |
| The person seen | physiotherapist | clinician     |

Shared labels: "Book", "My bookings", "Clinic bookings", "Sign in", "Confirmed" and "Cancelled".

- **WR-9** Use each clinic's own words consistently, and keep each clinic's user guide (`docs/user-guide/`) in step
  when a change alters what clients see or do or the words they read.

## Examples

Do: "You're booked in for Tue, 20 Oct 2026, 11:15 with Alex." Don't: "Booking successful!!"

Do: "Sorry, that slot has just been booked. Please choose another." Don't: "Error 409: booking transaction failed."

# Writing for BookIt

## Voice

Friendly, calm and direct, like a good receptionist. We speak to the reader as "you" and about the clinic by its name.

## Rules

- **UK English**: "cancelled", "organise", "colour".
- **Short and plain**: everyday words, short sentences, no technical terms. "Slot" is fine in the app; "database",
  "error code" or "server" are not.
- **Sentence case** for headings and buttons: "Confirm your booking", not "Confirm Your Booking".
- **Buttons say what they do**, starting with a verb: "Confirm booking", "Cancel booking", "Save settings". Never
  "OK" or "Submit".
- **Messages after an action** are one short sentence ending in a full stop: "Booking cancelled."
- **Problems** follow the pattern: what happened, then what to do. Say "Sorry" when it is not the person's fault:
  "Sorry, that slot has just been booked. Please choose another."
- **Dates and times** look like "Tue, 20 Oct 2026, 11:15", in the clinic's time zone.
- **No em dashes.** Use a full stop, a comma or a colon instead.

## Each clinic's words

Use the words each clinic already uses with its clients:

|                 | Acme Physio     | Beacon Dental |
| --------------- | --------------- | ------------- |
| An appointment  | session         | appointment   |
| The person seen | physiotherapist | clinician     |

Keep each clinic's user guide (`docs/user-guide/`) in step when a change alters what clients see or do.
